# Outreach Swipe File v2 — LocalPulse AI (Storm/Emergency Trades Focus)

Adaptasi dari `penetrasi pasar.docx`, disesuaikan ke offer LocalPulse AI (site + AI search visibility + missed-call recovery) dan niche prioritas: **roofing, HVAC, electrical, garage door** di kota-kota storm-belt (Tampa, Orlando, Houston, New Orleans, Baltimore, Charlotte, Myrtle Beach).

Prinsip sama seperti sebelumnya: **value dulu, link belakangan**. Jangan kirim link di kontak pertama. CTA akhir bukan lagi "Loom + invoice" — sekarang closingnya ke **Free 60-Second Video Audit** (sesuai landing page), lalu preview link sebelum bayar.

Ganti `[Business Name]`, `[Name]`, `[Niche]`, `[City]` dengan data asli dari `leads.csv` (kolom Business Name / Niche / City Searched).

Prioritaskan prospek dengan **Priority Score tertinggi** di leads.csv (no website / slow site / few reviews) — mereka yang paling gampang closing karena gap-nya paling kelihatan.

---

## 1. FACEBOOK GROUPS

Cari grup: "[City] roofing contractors", "[City] storm damage restoration", "[Niche] business owners [City]", grup Chamber of Commerce lokal. Cek rules grup dulu — banyak yang larang solicitation langsung.

### A. Post value-first (bukan pitch)
> Been checking how roofing/HVAC companies in [City] show up right now — during storm season, when search volume spikes overnight.
>
> More than half either don't show up when people search "emergency roof repair near me" on Google or ChatGPT, or their site takes 4+ seconds to load on mobile — and during a storm surge, people don't wait, they call the next name.
>
> Not selling anything, just surprised how many good companies are invisible exactly when demand is highest. Anyone else tracking this on their own listings?

Kuncian: kalau ada yang komentar "how do you check that?" — itu izin masuk ke DM personal.

### B. DM pertama (setelah komentar, atau cold ke halaman bisnis)
> Hi [Name] — saw [Business Name] while I was looking at roofing/HVAC companies in [City]. Quick question: when a storm hits and calls spike, does your team ever miss some, or is that pretty well covered?

(Tunggu balasan apa pun — itu pintu masuk ke follow-up.)

### C. Follow-up setelah mereka balas
> Figured as much — I actually tested a few [niche] companies' phone lines and Google/AI visibility in [City] this week. Want me to show you what I found for [Business Name] specifically? I'll send a 60-second video, no cost, no strings attached.

## 2. LINKEDIN

Batas aman: max 15-20 connection request/hari untuk akun baru. Personalisasi tiap note.

### A. Connection request note (max ~300 karakter)
> Hi [Name] — I help storm-belt roofing/HVAC/electrical companies stop losing emergency calls when demand spikes. Ran a quick visibility check on a few [City] companies this week, thought you might find it interesting. Open to connecting?

### B. Pesan pertama setelah connect diterima
> Thanks for connecting! No pitch here — I tested [Business Name]'s Google/AI search visibility and phone response this week as part of a small project on storm-season readiness for [niche] companies. Want me to share what I found? Takes 2 minutes to watch.

### C. Post untuk inbound pasif (dipasang di profil)
> A roofing company in [storm-belt city] got 40 calls in the 6 hours after a storm hit. They answered 9.
>
> The other 31? Gone to the next name on Google.
>
> This is happening across the storm belt right now — call volume spikes 500-1000% in the first 24 hours after a major storm, and most companies are still running normal-day phone coverage.
>
> Most owners have no idea how many of those calls they're actually losing — until someone tests it.

(Ganti jadi cerita spesifik nyata begitu ada 1 hasil audit real.)

## THE UNIVERSAL CLOSE (dipakai di platform manapun)

Begitu ada yang bilang "yes, show me" / "go ahead":
> Awesome — I'll send over a 60-second video showing exactly what I found for [Business Name], plus what LocalPulse AI would look like live on your business. What's the best email, or should I just send it here?

Alur setelah itu (ikuti landing page):
1. Kirim video audit 60 detik (temuan: no website / slow site / missed-call gap + estimasi lead yang hilang).
2. Kalau tertarik → kirim **preview link** (site sudah jadi, nama bisnis mereka, belum live) — bukan Loom/audit report generik seperti versi lama.
3. Mereka approve → kirim invoice setup sesuai plan (Local Foundation $549+$97/mo, Smart Lead $967+$197/mo — *sweet spot*, atau Turnkey AI $2,400+$497/mo untuk yang mau eksklusivitas per kota/trade).
4. Domain connect + AI Assistant aktif setelah invoice cair (±15 menit).

## Catatan pemakaian dengan leads.csv

- Kolom `Site Status` = `NO WEBSITE` atau `SLOW`/`ERROR`/`UNREACHABLE` → pakai angle "invisible saat storm surge" (script A di atas).
- Kolom `Site Status` = `OK` tapi `Review Count` rendah → geser angle ke "visibility gap", bukan "site jelek".
- Isi kolom `Channel`, `Status`, `Script Used`, `Date Contacted` di leads.csv/tracker setiap kali kontak — supaya nggak dobel-DM prospek yang sama.
