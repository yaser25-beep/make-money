No. Your site is built and fully working on a private preview link first. You can click through it, test it on your phone, and see your real business name and content on it before you send a cent.

Does this replace my current phone number or booking system?

No. We connect to what you already use — your existing phone number stays the same, and the AI Assistant hands off to your booking or dispatch software rather than replacing it.

How fast is the build, really?

Most sites are built and ready to review on the preview link within 48 hours of us receiving your business details.

What's the difference between the three plans?

Local Foundation gets you a fast, mobile-first site with the basics. Smart Lead — our most popular plan — adds full AI search optimization, a 24/7 AI chat widget, and missed-call text-back so you stop losing leads after hours. Turnkey AI adds a full AI phone receptionist and exclusivity in your city.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract is required to start. You pay setup once, then a flat monthly rate for hosting, updates, and AI management.

What happens to my missed calls?

On Smart Lead and above, a missed call automatically triggers a text reply within seconds, and you get an instant alert — so you can follow up before the caller moves on to a competitor.

Is my business information safe?

Yes. Your business details are only used to build and operate your site and AI Assistant — never sold or shared.
